The objective

Objective

Build a trusted appliance brand identity, launch a high-converting Shopify store, expand across major marketplaces, increase product visibility and trust, and develop scalable acquisition and retention systems across Europe.

The challenge

Challenge

The brand operated in a crowded, price-sensitive appliance category where new entrants must prove performance, reliability, and utility before customers are willing to buy.

  • Brand identity was weak or inconsistent in a competitive category.
  • First-time buyers had limited trust in a new appliance brand.
  • Marketplace dependence created intense price competition.
  • There was no structured paid advertising or funnel-based acquisition system.
  • Product differentiation relied too heavily on features instead of customer outcomes.
  • Content did not clearly demonstrate real product value or usage.

The approach

Strategy

We built a structured e-commerce growth system around one principle: in home and kitchen appliances, purchase decisions are driven by trust, performance clarity, and proof of utility. Brand, store UX, marketplaces, content, influencers, and paid media were designed to reduce buyer hesitation.

The execution

Growth Phases

01

Brand Identity and Market Positioning

We created a performance-oriented brand identity tailored to the appliance category.

  • Positioned the brand around reliability, efficiency, and everyday utility.
  • Developed a logo, typography, color system, and brand guidelines.
  • Built messaging around product performance, durability, and practical household value.
  • Aligned brand expression for Shopify, marketplaces, content, and ads.
Impact

Trust perception improved among first-time buyers, reducing hesitation in a category where credibility directly affects conversion.

02

Shopify Store Design and Conversion Optimization

The Shopify store was built around problem-solution positioning rather than product listing alone.

  • Created conversion-focused UI and UX for utility products.
  • Wrote SEO-optimized, benefit-driven product descriptions.
  • Translated appliance features into customer benefits.
  • Added comparison-friendly layouts, guarantees, reviews, usage visuals, and mobile-first optimization.
Impact

Shopify became the core conversion hub, improving engagement and product page-to-checkout progression.

03

Multi-Channel Marketplace Expansion

We expanded the brand across Amazon, Bol.com, and Vinted alongside Shopify.

  • Optimized each platform with keyword-rich listings.
  • Created competitive pricing and platform-specific content formats.
  • Balanced brand consistency with marketplace performance requirements.
  • Reduced dependence on a single channel by building multiple revenue pathways.
Impact

The brand gained diversified revenue across European marketplaces and improved overall sales stability.

04

Content, Social Media, and Influencer Proof

A content ecosystem demonstrated product utility through real usage instead of abstract promotion.

  • Built content across Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X, and YouTube.
  • Created product demos, before-and-after scenarios, kitchen utility ideas, and educational usage content.
  • Used micro-creators to show real appliance use in kitchens and households.
  • Focused social proof on authenticity, performance, and customer confidence.
Impact

Real-world usage content improved product credibility, engagement, and purchase intent.

05

Paid Ads and Conversion Optimization

We built a full-funnel paid advertising system across Meta and Google Ads.

  • Launched cold audience acquisition campaigns.
  • Created retargeting for product viewers and cart abandoners.
  • Built high-intent Google search campaigns.
  • Optimized seasonal promotions, funnel drop-offs, and conversion paths using performance data.
Impact

The business gained a more predictable acquisition engine with improving conversion efficiency and stronger ROAS over time.

The outcome

Results

  • Built a structured appliance brand identity optimized for European markets.
  • Launched a high-converting Shopify store focused on utility-driven UX.
  • Expanded distribution across Shopify, Amazon, Bol.com, and Vinted.
  • Established consistent multi-platform product visibility across Europe.
  • Increased engagement through a structured social media content strategy.
  • Executed influencer campaigns that generated real product usage content.
  • Launched a full-funnel Meta and Google Ads acquisition system.
  • Improved conversion rates through continuous CRO optimization.
  • Built a scalable multi-channel e-commerce revenue ecosystem.

What the work revealed

Key Insights

Appliance purchases need proof

Customers need to see functionality, reliability, and utility before buying from a new brand.

Marketplace diversification matters

Amazon and Bol.com drive volume while Shopify builds brand equity and margin control.

Demonstration content converts

Real usage videos outperform generic promotional content in utility-driven categories.

Funnels beat direct selling

Full-funnel paid media improves efficiency for products that require consideration.

Brand and performance must align

Strong branding creates trust while performance systems create scalable revenue.

Strategic takeaway

A home and kitchen appliance brand can move from fragmented selling to a scalable e-commerce business when brand identity, conversion infrastructure, marketplace expansion, social proof, and performance marketing work together across the full customer journey.
