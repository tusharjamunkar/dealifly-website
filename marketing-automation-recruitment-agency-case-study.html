Healthcare Recruitment Automation

Marketing Automating Set-up For Recruitment Agency

Dealifly was engaged to replace the client's manual candidate handling with a faster, more consistent screening process.

Client Profile

Industry: Healthcare Recruitment and Staffing
Business Model: Placement of healthcare professionals with care providers and healthcare organizations
Market: Netherlands
Candidate Source: Indeed job postings

The Objective

Objective

Dealifly was engaged to replace the client's manual candidate handling with a faster, more consistent screening process. The engagement focused on:

  • Filtering incoming applications automatically against the client's requirements
  • Contacting qualified candidates immediately, without recruiter effort
  • Gathering screening information through WhatsApp, the channel candidates already use
  • Booking first interview calls directly into recruiters' calendars
  • Freeing recruiters to spend their time on conversations with suitable candidates

The Challenge

The Challenge

The client received a steady flow of applications through Indeed, but every step after that was manual. Recruiters reviewed each application by hand, then contacted candidates one by one on WhatsApp to ask screening questions and arrange a call. The client faced several obstacles:

  • Large volumes of applications to read and sort manually
  • Slow response times, which risked losing candidates to other agencies
  • Repetitive screening questions asked again and again by recruiters
  • Inconsistent filtering, depending on who reviewed the application
  • Interview scheduling done through long back-and-forth messages
  • Recruiter time spent on unsuitable candidates instead of strong ones

In healthcare recruitment, good candidates are in demand and often apply to several employers at once. The agency that responds first usually gets the first conversation.

Why Hiring Was Slowing Down

The client was not short of applicants. The delay came from what happened after an application arrived. Each candidate had to be reviewed, messaged, questioned, and scheduled by hand, so response times depended on how busy the recruiters were. Qualified candidates waited, some lost interest or accepted other offers, and recruiters spent much of their day on administrative follow-up rather than on interviews and placements.

The Approach

Approach

Dealifly's approach rested on one principle: speed and consistency decide who wins healthcare candidates. Every applicant should get a fast response and the same fair screening, and recruiters should only step in when a candidate is ready to talk.

The engagement was organized around three areas of work:

  • 1. Automated application filtering
  • 2. WhatsApp screening conversations
  • 3. Interview booking and recruiter handoff

Each area was designed to remove manual steps while keeping the candidate experience personal and professional.

The Execution

Execution

01

1. Automated Application Filtering

Dealifly set up an automated workflow that captured applications from Indeed as they arrived and checked them against the client's criteria, such as role, qualifications, location, availability, and language. Applications were sorted automatically, so suitable candidates moved forward immediately while those who did not meet the basic requirements were handled without recruiter time.

Impact:

Recruiters no longer had to read every application. Filtering became consistent, and qualified candidates were identified in minutes rather than days.

02

2. WhatsApp Screening Conversations

Candidates who passed the first filter were contacted automatically on WhatsApp. A chatbot opened the conversation, introduced the agency, and asked the client's standard screening questions about experience, certifications, preferred working hours, notice period, and expectations. Responses were recorded and used to filter candidates further, so only those who genuinely matched the role progressed.

Impact:

Candidates received an immediate reply on a channel they already use, and recruiters received complete screening information before ever speaking to them.

03

3. Interview Booking and Recruiter Handoff

Candidates who met the criteria were offered available time slots for a first interview call directly in the chat. Once they chose a time, the booking was confirmed and added to the recruiter's calendar, with the candidate's screening answers attached for reference.

Automated reminders reduced missed calls.

Impact:

The back-and-forth of arranging interviews disappeared. Recruiters began each call with the candidate's details in front of them and could focus on assessing fit.

The Outcome

Key Results

  • A fully automated path from Indeed application to booked interview call
  • Consistent, criteria-based filtering of every applicant
  • Immediate WhatsApp contact with qualified candidates
  • Standardized screening information collected before the first call
  • Interview scheduling handled without manual messaging
  • Significantly less recruiter time spent on administrative follow-up

Key Learnings

Key Insights

1. Speed wins candidates.

In healthcare recruitment, the first agency to respond often secures the conversation.

2. Consistent screening is fairer and more reliable.

Every candidate is assessed against the same criteria.

3. Candidates respond on the channel they already use.

WhatsApp conversations feel natural and keep candidates engaged.

4. Recruiter time is best spent with qualified candidates.

Removing administrative steps lets recruiters focus on relationships and placements.

5. Scheduling should not be a conversation.

Letting candidates book their own interview removes delay and drop-off.

Conclusion

This engagement shows how a healthcare recruitment agency in the Netherlands can move from manual application handling to an automated, candidate-friendly screening process.

By filtering applications automatically, continuing the conversation on WhatsApp, and booking interviews without manual effort, Dealifly helped the client respond faster, screen more consistently, and give its recruiters more time for the conversations that lead to placements.
