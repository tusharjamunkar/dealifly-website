Luxury Fragrance & E-Commerce

Ecommerce Marketing - Luxury Fragrance Brand

Dealifly was engaged to give the brand a clear luxury identity and make its online store easier to shop and more convincing to first-time buyers.

Client Profile

Industry: Luxury Fragrance and Home Scenting (E-Commerce)
Business Model: Direct-to-consumer online sales
Market: Netherlands, Belgium
Product Category: Personal fragrances and home fragrances, including perfumes and diffusers

The Objective

Objective

Dealifly was engaged to give the brand a clear luxury identity and make its online store easier to shop and more convincing to first-time buyers. The engagement focused on:

  • Defining the brand as a luxury house for personal and home fragrance
  • Narrowing the product range to its core strengths
  • Refining pricing to match the brand's luxury positioning
  • Organizing the website so customers could find the right fragrance quickly
  • Building credibility with new customers
  • Running advertising that reflected the brand's premium image

The Challenge

The Challenge

The brand had strong products but a diluted identity. As it grew, it introduced new categories such as jewelry and skincare, which pulled it away from what it did best. The client faced several obstacles:

  • A product range that spread across unrelated categories
  • Unclear brand identity, with customers unsure whether it was a fragrance house or a general lifestyle store
  • Pricing that did not consistently reflect a luxury position
  • Inconsistent brand communication across the website and social channels
  • A fragrance catalog that was hard to browse, leaving customers without a simple way to find scents they would enjoy
  • Limited credibility signals for shoppers new to the brand

In luxury, clarity is part of the product. A brand that tries to be everything struggles to be seen as exceptional at anything.

Why Customers Weren't Choosing the Brand

Shoppers who reached the website met a wide and unfocused range. Jewelry and skincare sat alongside fragrance, which weakened the impression of a specialist house. Fragrances were not grouped in a way that matched how people choose scents, so a customer who loved vanilla had no easy path to the vanilla options, and someone drawn to amber or oud had to search through the full catalog. Choosing a luxury fragrance online already requires trust, and the extra effort made it easier to leave than to buy.

The Approach

Approach

Dealifly's approach rested on one principle: luxury is built on focus. A brand earns premium pricing and customer trust by being clear about what it is, what it offers, and who it is for.

The engagement was organized around four areas of work:

  • 1. Brand positioning and range focus
  • 2. Pricing and credibility
  • 3. Website structure and customer experience
  • 4. Advertising
  • 5. Marketplace listing

Each area was designed to make the brand's identity unmistakable and the path to purchase effortless.

The Execution

Execution

01

1. Brand Positioning and Range Focus

Dealifly defined the brand as a luxury house specializing in personal and home fragrance. As part of this, the jewelry and skincare categories were removed so the brand could concentrate on perfumes, diffusers, and related home scenting products.

Messaging was rewritten across the website and social channels to state clearly what the brand is, what it offers, and what makes the experience distinctive.

Impact:

The brand now presents a single, confident identity. Customers understand immediately that they are dealing with a specialist luxury fragrance house.

02

2. Pricing and Credibility

Pricing was reviewed and adjusted to align with the brand's luxury positioning and the expectations of its target customers.

Credibility was strengthened through consistent brand communication, refined product presentation, and trust signals such as customer reviews and testimonials placed throughout the store,trustpilot and google.

Impact:

Prices felt more coherent with the brand's premium image, and first-time buyers had clearer reasons to trust the brand and complete a purchase.

03

3. Website Structure and Customer Experience

The website was reorganized around how people actually choose fragrance. Products were grouped by scent family, so customers who love vanilla could explore vanilla fragrances in one place, and those drawn to amber, oud, or other profiles could do the same.

Personal and home fragrance were clearly separated, with consistent product pages and descriptions that helped customers understand each scent.

Impact:

Browsing became simpler and more intuitive, helping customers find scents they would enjoy with less effort and making the purchase decision easier.

04

4. Advertising

Dealifly developed meta advertising that reflected the brand's refined positioning, with premium visuals and messaging focused on scent, atmosphere, and the brand's luxury identity.

Campaigns directed customers to the reorganized website, where the clearer structure supported the path from first click to purchase.

Impact:

Advertising and website now told the same story, so the traffic the brand attracted met an experience that matched what the ads promised.

The Outcome

Key Results

  • A clearly defined luxury brand identity focused on personal and home fragrance
  • A refined product range, with non-core categories removed
  • Pricing aligned with the brand's luxury positioning
  • A website organized by scent family for easier discovery
  • Stronger credibility through consistent communication and customer proof
  • Advertising consistent with the brand's premium identity

Key Learnings

Key Insights

1. Focus builds luxury.

A specialist brand is more credible than one that sells everything.

2. Clarity drives conversion.

Customers buy when they quickly understand what a brand is and what it offers.

3. Pricing is part of positioning.

Prices must support the brand's premium image.

4. Fragrance should be organized by how people choose it.

Grouping by scent family helps customers explore with confidence.

5. Advertising works best when the website delivers on its promise.

Consistency between the two turns attention into sales.

Conclusion

This engagement shows how a luxury fragrance brand can strengthen its position by narrowing its focus, communicating clearly, and making its online experience easier to navigate.

By refining its range, aligning its pricing, structuring its website around how customers choose scents, and supporting it with consistent advertising, Dealifly helped the client present itself as a distinct luxury fragrance house, ready to grow with greater confidence.
